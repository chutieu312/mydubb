# MyDubb glossary: English terms that stay in English in the Vietnamese translation.
#
# Only terms listed here are protected. MyDubb does NOT detect unknown technical terms
# automatically; a term that is missing here may be translated (or left alone) by the
# translation service, depending on the service.
#
# Format (one term per line, reviewed through Git):
#   * Lines starting with "#" and blank lines are ignored.
#   * Matching ignores capitalization, and the text keeps its own spelling: "Neural Network"
#     in the transcript stays "Neural Network"; "neural network" stays lowercase.
#   * A line starting with "=" matches that exact capitalization only. Use it for terms that
#     are also ordinary words in another case (REST vs "rest").
#   * Terms match whole words only ("API" is not matched inside "capital"). A plural "s" is
#     part of the match ("APIs", "neural networks"), so list the singular form.
#   * Words in a multi-word term may be separated by any whitespace.
#   * When terms overlap, the longest match wins ("convolutional neural network" beats
#     "neural network").
#
# What belongs here (policy for ambiguous words):
#   * Add specific terms: acronyms, product and language names, and multi-word phrases.
#   * Do NOT add everyday words that have a normal meaning outside the field, such as
#     class, model, cell, function, table, key, value, string, object, field, layer, node,
#     tree, thread, memory, spring, go, rust, swift, ruby, java (island), transformer or
#     "it". Keeping them in English in every context would damage ordinary sentences. When
#     such a word is needed, add the specific phrase instead ("Spring Boot", "loss
#     function").
#   * Prefer the phrase over its parts: "gradient descent", not "gradient".
