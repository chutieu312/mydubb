MyDubb Chrome extension privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Operator: Can Nguyen

Support and privacy contact: support@mydubb.com

Scope

This policy covers the MyDubb Chrome extension and the requests it sends to the MyDubb service at mydubb.com. It is not a privacy policy for the MyDubb website as a whole: it does not describe website features the extension does not use, such as the studio page, guest use or importing captions from a YouTube link.

MyDubb is an independent service. It is not made by, endorsed by or connected with Udemy, Microsoft or Google.

What the extension is for

The extension takes English transcript text that you select on an Udemy lecture page and, when you ask, turns it into audio in another language with your MyDubb account, so you can listen next to the lecture video.

What the extension handles in your browser

"Session storage" below is Chrome's session storage for the extension. Chrome clears it when the browser quits, and Udemy pages cannot read it.

  • The text you select. Read when you choose Send to MyDubb on an Udemy page. It is shown in the side panel and kept in session storage. It is sent to MyDubb only when you choose Generate audio.
  • Your choices. Start times, language, voice and speed. They are kept in session storage and sent to MyDubb with the text when you choose Generate audio.
  • The address and title of the current lecture. Read from the Udemy page in that tab, to tell which lecture the panel belongs to and to show its title. They stay in your browser and are not sent to MyDubb. This is not your browsing history: the extension has no access to Chrome's history, and its page script runs on www.udemy.com only.
  • The state of the lecture video. Its time, duration, speed, and whether it is paused or muted. Read to show the video controls and to keep audio and video together. It stays in your browser. When new audio loads, the extension pauses the lecture video and moves it to where your passage starts. With Sync with video on, it also mutes the video and changes its speed, and restores them when Sync turns off.
  • Pointer and key presses, only while Sync with video is on. The extension notes the time of your latest pointer press or key press on the page, to tell a speed you picked from one the site put back, and so keep the video coordinated. Only that time is held, in memory. Which key you pressed, what you typed and where you pressed are not recorded, and nothing about it is stored or sent. This is not keystroke logging or analytics. The floating player's own controls also respond to presses on them.
  • The translation and the audio. When a job finishes, the panel fetches them from MyDubb. The translation is kept in session storage together with the account, connection and lecture it belongs to, so the panel can show it again. The audio is held in memory by the extension's player and is not saved by the extension.
  • Your MyDubb sign-in. When you choose Connect MyDubb, you type your password on mydubb.com, not in the extension. The extension receives a sign-in token and your account's e-mail and name, keeps them in session storage, and sends the token to MyDubb with its requests.

The extension has no analytics or advertising code. It does not read your Udemy account details or your cookies.

Requests the extension sends to MyDubb

Your text and choices are sent only when you choose Generate audio. Some other requests happen without a click, and they carry no transcript text:

  • Every time the side panel opens, it asks MyDubb for the public list of languages and voices, with no sign-in token. Like any request, it reaches MyDubb with your network address.
  • If the panel holds a sign-in token, it asks MyDubb whether the token is still valid.
  • If a job was started in that tab and the panel is closed and opened again, or while a job runs, the panel asks MyDubb for the job's status, then its translation and its audio file, and keeps doing so until the job ends or you leave the lecture. These requests carry your token and the job's id, not your text.
  • Connect MyDubb and Disconnect contact MyDubb when you press them. The sign-in window is the MyDubb website, which sets the cookies it needs for signing in.

What MyDubb does with a request

  • Microsoft Azure. MyDubb sends your text to Azure Translator (text translation), and the translation to Azure Speech (real-time text to speech). Microsoft's own terms and privacy statement govern what Microsoft does with it. Microsoft's documentation states that text translation "doesn’t store customer data" (Azure Translator), that "Microsoft doesn't retain or store the text that you provide with the real-time synthesis text to speech API", and that "Microsoft doesn't store audio or video content generated with the real-time synthesis API" (text to speech). These are Microsoft's statements about those services, not promises by MyDubb.
  • Your transcript on the server. It is held in memory while the job runs and is not written to MyDubb's database. The server attempts to remove temporary working files when a job finishes. An interrupted process or a cleanup failure can leave files behind.
  • The translation and the audio on the server. They are saved as files so the panel can fetch them. They stop being available one hour after the job completes. A cleanup that runs periodically then removes the files, so they can stay on the disk for some time after that hour; a server restart can also remove them sooner.
  • A record of each job. Status, voice, language, start times, speed, length of the text, the providers used, any error, the times of the job and the account that made it. It does not contain your text. It is kept in MyDubb's database after the result has expired, and it is not deleted automatically today.
  • A usage record of each job. The client (extension or website), the kind of access, language, voice, speed, timing mode, the providers used, the number of characters sent, durations, the size of the audio, the outcome and any error code, and for each request to a provider its outcome and timing. It is used to measure use and cost. It contains no text, name, e-mail or account id, but it is not anonymous: it carries the job's id, so anyone who can read MyDubb's database can match it to the job record and, through that, to the account. MyDubb's configuration keeps usage records for 180 days; a periodic cleanup then removes them, so removal can happen somewhat later. Until then they stay even if the job record or the account is removed.
  • A list of technical terms. English technical terms found in translations, with counts and when they were first and last seen, without the text, the job or the account they came from. This list is not deleted automatically.
  • Logs. MyDubb's application logs hold counts, status codes and ids, not your text or translations. The web server's access log records technical details of each request, including your network address, in logs kept by MyDubb's host. Render documents a log retention period of 7 days for MyDubb's workspace plan (Hobby). That describes Render's log retention by plan; it is not a promise about every internal log of a provider, or about exports or backups.

Your account

Access is by invitation. MyDubb keeps your e-mail address, your name, your identifier at Amazon Cognito, your account's status and role, and the time of your last sign-in. Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito, and MyDubb does not store your password. The extension's token lasts at most 12 hours; the server stores only a hash of it, and a periodic cleanup removes that record after the token has expired or ended. Account records, and records of a change to an account's role, are not deleted automatically.

Who can see your data at MyDubb

People with the administrator role at MyDubb can open a Usage & Costs page on the MyDubb website, which shows usage records and reference costs per job. Its API reads the usage records and each job's status and error code. It returns the job's id, but not an e-mail address, an account id, a transcript or a translation.

The operator reads your content only when you provide it yourself for support, or when the law requires it.

Service providers

  • Microsoft (Azure Translator and Azure Speech): your text and its translation.
  • Render: hosts the MyDubb application, its temporary result files and its logs. Requests reach Render through a Cloudflare network edge.
  • Neon: hosts MyDubb's database.
  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon Cognito): account sign-in.

This policy does not promise that data stays in a particular region or country.

How your data is not used

MyDubb does not sell user data. It does not transfer user data to others except in the cases the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy allows, such as the service providers above that are needed to provide the extension's purpose. It does not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's purpose, for advertising, or to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Ending the connection and removing data from your browser

  • Disconnect removes the token from your browser and ends it on the server.
  • Clear empties the transcript text in the panel and removes the saved draft and any selection waiting to be shown. It does not remove the translation or the audio already loaded in the player, the job being followed or your start times.
  • Your choices are removed when you close the tab or quit the browser. Moving to another lecture blanks the start times.
  • The audio (kept in memory only) is discarded when a newer result replaces it, when you move to another lecture, reload or leave the lecture page, or close the tab, and when you Disconnect or connect another account.
  • The saved translation of the loaded audio is removed when a newer result has loaded and replaces it, when you move to another lecture or off the lecture pages, when you Disconnect or the connection ends or expires, when another account connects, when you close the tab, and when the browser quits. A request that MyDubb refuses, or a job or download that fails, does not remove the earlier audio or its translation. Reloading the page keeps the saved translation.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored in your browser.

Asking about your data

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to support@mydubb.com. Please write from the e-mail address of your MyDubb account if you can, and do not send transcripts, passwords or tokens. The operator verifies who is asking and handles the request as far as is practically possible and as the law requires.

MyDubb has no automatic deletion of account or job records today. This policy does not promise that all data can be deleted, or a fixed response time. Nothing here limits any right you have under the law that applies to you.

Security

The extension talks to MyDubb over HTTPS only. Your token is kept in the extension's session storage, and the extension never sends your password or cookies. No system is perfectly secure, and this policy makes no promise beyond this description.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

The use of information received by the MyDubb extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the text on this page and the Last updated date are changed. Additional notice is given when the law requires it.
